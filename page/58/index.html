About Siddhast

Consulting & technology for innovation-led growth

Established in 2005 and headquartered in Jhansi, Uttar Pradesh, Siddhast Intellectual Property Innovation Pvt. Ltd. partners with businesses, innovators, startups, institutions, and enterprises across India and the SAARC region.

We deliver integrated consulting across Information Technology, IT Asset Management, Intellectual Property Rights, innovation management, business advisory, and digital asset protection — helping organizations modernize operations, protect innovation, and create scalable value.
